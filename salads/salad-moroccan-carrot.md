# Moroccan Carrot Salad

## Ingredients

* 500 g carrots, coarsely grated
* 1 red pepper, finely diced
* 50 g raisins or dried cranberries
* small handful fresh parsley or coriander, chopped
* 2 tbsp flaked almonds, toasted (optional)

## Dressing

* 2 tbsp fresh orange juice
* 1 1/2 tbsp lemon juice
* 2 tbsp extra virgin olive oil
* 1 to 2 tsp honey
* pinch ground cumin (don't use too much!)
* 1/4 tsp ground coriander
* pinch of ground cinnamon
* 1/2 tsp salt
* black pepper

## Method

1. Put the grated carrots, red pepper and dried fruit into a large bowl.
2. Whisk the orange juice, lemon juice, olive oil, honey, cumin, coriander, cinnamon, salt and black pepper together in a small bowl.
3. Pour the dressing over the salad and toss well.
4. Refrigerate for 30 to 60 minutes to allow the carrots to soften slightly and the dried fruit to plump up.
5. Add the parsley or coriander and toasted almonds, if using, and toss again.
6. Taste and add more honey, lemon juice, salt or black pepper if needed.
7. Serve chilled or at room temperature.
