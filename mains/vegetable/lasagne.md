# Saturday Vegetable Lasagne

## Ingredients

- 1 large aubergine, cut into 1.5 cm cubes
- 2 large red peppers, deseeded and chopped
- 3 T olive oil
- salt and black pepper

- 1 T olive oil
- 1 large onion, finely chopped
- 1 large carrot, finely diced
- 3 garlic cloves, crushed
- 2 T tomato purée
- 150 ml white wine (or vegetable stock)
- 2 x 400 g tins chopped tomatoes
- 1 x 400 g tin green lentils, drained and rinsed
- 1 t dried oregano
- handful fresh basil, chopped
- 1 t soy sauce (optional)
- salt and black pepper

- 75 g butter
- 75 g plain flour
- 750 ml milk
- 150 g mature cheddar, grated
- salt and black pepper

- lasagne sheets
- 125 g mozzarella, drained and torn
- 50 g mature cheddar, grated

## Method

1. Heat oven to 200 °C (180 °C fan).
2. Toss aubergine and peppers with olive oil, salt and pepper. Spread over two trays and roast for 25–30 minutes until tender and lightly browned.
3. Meanwhile, heat olive oil in a large saucepan. Add onion and carrot and cook for about 8 minutes until softened.
4. Add garlic and tomato purée. Cook for another 2 minutes, stirring.
5. Add white wine and simmer until mostly reduced. Stir in chopped tomatoes and oregano. Simmer uncovered for 25–30 minutes, stirring occasionally, until rich and thick but still spoonable.
6. Stir in drained lentils and roasted vegetables. Simmer for another 5–10 minutes. Add basil and optional soy sauce. Season to taste. If the sauce is very thick, loosen it with a splash of water or stock so the pasta can cook properly.
7. For the cheese sauce, melt butter in a saucepan. Stir in flour and cook for 2 minutes. Gradually whisk in milk. Simmer, stirring, until smooth and thickened. Remove from heat and stir in 150 g cheddar. Season to taste.
8. Reduce oven to 180 °C (160 °C fan). Lightly grease a deep 30 x 20 cm baking dish.
9. Spread a thin layer of the tomato and vegetable sauce over the bottom. Add lasagne sheets, then one-third of the remaining tomato sauce and one-third of the cheese sauce. Repeat to make three pasta layers, ensuring the sheets are well covered in sauce. Finish with cheese sauce.
10. Scatter mozzarella and the remaining 50 g cheddar over the top. Bake for 40–45 minutes until golden, bubbling and the pasta is tender when pierced with a knife. Cover loosely with foil if the top browns too quickly.
11. Leave to stand for 15 minutes before cutting and serving.

Note: Check that the cheeses are made with vegetarian rennet if needed. The tomato and lentil sauce can be made the day before; cool promptly and refrigerate.
