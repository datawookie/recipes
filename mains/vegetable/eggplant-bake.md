# Eggplant, Blue Cheese & Salami Bake

## Ingredients

- 2 large eggplants
- 1 onion
- 2 cloves garlic
- 1 red pepper (sliced)
- shredded salami
- 1 packet cream of chicken soup
- 400–500 ml milk
- 100–150 g blue cheese
- 50–75 g yellow cheese (grated)
- olive oil
- black pepper
- breadcrumbs (optional)

## Method

1. Slice the eggplants into rounds about 1 cm thick. Brush with a little olive oil and roast at 200 °C (180 °C fan) for around 20–25 minutes, turning once, until soft and browned.
2. Slice the onion and fry in a little oil until soft. Add the garlic and cook for another minute.
3. Make the chicken soup with 400–500 ml milk, using less liquid than specified on the packet so that the sauce is thick.
4. Crumble about two-thirds of the blue cheese into the soup and stir until melted. Season with black pepper.
5. Layer eggplant in a baking dish. Add some onion, sliced red pepper and shredded salami, then spoon over some of the blue cheese sauce.
6. Repeat the layers, finishing with the remaining sauce.
7. Crumble the remaining blue cheese over the top. Sprinkle with grated yellow cheese and breadcrumbs if using.
8. Bake at 200 °C (180 °C fan) for around 20–25 minutes, until bubbling and browned on top.
9. Leave to stand for 5–10 minutes before serving.
