# Loaded Potato Skins

## Ingredients

- 4 baking potatoes (large)
- 1 T oil
- 250 g bacon (chopped into small pieces)
- spring onions (finely chopped)
- 50 g cream cheese
- 1 egg
- 150 g Cheddar cheese
- salt
- pepper
- sour cream (optional)

## Method

- Heat the oven to 200°C.
- Smear the potatoes with oil and then sprinkle salt liberally.
- Bake in oven for 1 hour, turning after 30 minutes.
- Allow potaoes to cool slightly.
- Cut the potatoes in half and scoop the middle out into a bowl.
- Put the potato shells onto a baking tray and return to the oven for 10 minutes.
- Heat the oil in a frying pan and fry the bacon until crispy.
- Add the bacon, spring onions, cream cheese, egg, half of the grated cheddar and plenty of salt and pepper into the bowl.
- Mash everything together well.
- Remove the potato shells from the oven and share the mixture between them.
- Top with the remaining cheese.
- Return to the oven for about 10 minutes until the cheese is bubbling and crispy.
- Serve with the sour cream and extra chopped spring onions.
