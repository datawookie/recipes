# Quiche Fillings

## Cauliflower Cheese & Bacon

### Ingredients

* 1 small cauliflower, broken into small florets
* 100 g thick-cut unsmoked streaky bacon, roughly chopped
* 3 large free-range eggs
* 284 ml double cream
* 175 g cheese, finely grated (use a mixture of Parmesan, cheddar, Gruyère or Emmental)
* 2 t snipped chives
* Nutmeg
* Salt
* Black pepper

### Method

1. Boil the cauliflower in salted water for 5 minutes, then drain well and leave to cool.
2. Put the bacon in a cold non-stick frying pan and cook gently for 7–8 minutes, until the fat has rendered and the bacon has coloured but is not crisp. Leave to cool.
3. Beat the eggs with the cream, cheese, chives, grated nutmeg, salt and black pepper. Stir in the cooled cauliflower and bacon.
4. Pour the filling into the prepared pastry case.
5. Bake for about 30 minutes, until the pastry is golden and the filling is just set.
6. Leave to stand for 30 minutes before serving warm.

## Mushroom

### Ingredients

* mushrooms
* 1 onion
* garlic
* 1½ T olive oil
* 3 large eggs
* double cream
* ½ t mustard powder
* ½ t salt
* ¼ t freshly ground black pepper
* 1 cup cheese (grated)

### Method

1. Clean and slice the mushrooms. Peel and thinly slice the onion, then finely chop the garlic.
2. Heat 1 tbsp olive oil in a frying pan over medium-high heat. Add the mushrooms, season lightly with salt and pepper, and cook for 11–14 minutes, stirring regularly, until their moisture has evaporated and they begin to brown.
3. Add the garlic and cook for another minute. Transfer the mushrooms and garlic to a bowl.
4. Add the remaining ½ tbsp olive oil to the pan. Cook the onion for 5–7 minutes, until soft and lightly browned. Add it to the mushrooms and mix well.
5. Whisk together the eggs, cream, mustard powder, salt and pepper.
6. Spread the mushroom and onion mixture over the prepared pastry case, then scatter the Gruyère over the top.
7. Pour in the egg mixture.
8. Bake at 170 °C fan / 190 °C conventional for about 25 minutes, until the filling is golden and completely set.
9. Leave to cool for at least 10–20 minutes before slicing. Serve warm or at room temperature.

## Tuna & Sweetcorn

### Ingredients

* 1 × tin tuna (drained)
* 1 × tin sweetcorn (~285 g drained)
* 1 small onion, finely chopped
* 100 g mature cheddar, grated
* 4 large eggs
* 240 ml double cream
* 60 ml whole milk
* 1 t Dijon mustard
* 1 t dried thyme, optional
* 2 t fresh parsley, chopped
* pinch salt
* ground black pepper

### Method

1. Drain the sweetcorn thoroughly. Spread it over kitchen paper and pat it dry to remove as much moisture as possible.
2. Sauté the onion according to the original recipe. Add the sweetcorn for the final 2–3 minutes and cook until the mixture looks dry and the corn begins to colour slightly.
3. Whisk together the eggs, double cream, milk, Dijon mustard, salt and pepper.
4. Distribute the tuna, onion and sweetcorn mixture, grated cheese and parsley evenly over the blind-baked pastry case.
5. Pour over the egg mixture. Do not fill the pastry completely to the rim. Reserve any excess rather than allowing the filling to overflow.
6. Bake at 180 °C (fan) for approximately 30–35 minutes, until the edges are firm and the centre has only a slight wobble.
7. Leave the quiche to rest for at least 10 minutes before slicing.
