# Quiche Base

## Ingredients

* 225 g plain flour, plus extra for rolling
* 140 g cold unsalted butter, roughly cubed
* 1 large egg
* 2–3 tbsp ice-cold water

## Method

1. Pulse the flour with a pinch of salt in a food processor. Add the butter and pulse until the mixture resembles breadcrumbs.
2. Mix the egg with 2 tbsp ice-cold water. 🚨 For light and flaky pastry, use only the egg yolk. Include the albumen for a stiffer, crunchier pastry.
3. Add the egg mixture to the flour and butter, then process until the pastry comes together. Add a little more water only if needed.
4. Shape the pastry into a thick disc, wrap it and chill for 30 minutes.
5. Roll the pastry out thinly and use it to line a 23 cm round tart tin, about 4 cm deep. Trim the edges and chill for another 30 minutes.
6. Heat the oven to 170 °C fan / 190 °C conventional / gas 5. Line the pastry case with foil and baking beans, then blind bake for 12–15 minutes.
7. Remove the foil and baking beans, then bake for another 5–7 minutes, until the pastry is dry and lightly golden.
